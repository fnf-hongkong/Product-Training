# MLB Product Training

Store-facing product training decks for MLB Hong Kong & Macau.

## FW26

**RTW — Part 1 (pages 1–40):**
https://fnf-hongkong.github.io/Product-Training/FW26/mlb-fw26-product-training-RTW.html

Covers the IMC plan, the FW26 buy strategy, the five monthly collections, and the
TS / WJ / WP / SK category run through to the Part 1 recap.

**ACC — headwear, shoes, bags & etc (33 slides):**
https://fnf-hongkong.github.io/Product-Training/FW26/mlb-fw26-product-training-ACC.html

Covers the FW26 ACC buy strategy, a profile handbook and a product matrix for each of the
three pillars, the headwear and bag collections, the cap fit guide, mix & match, and a
launch calendar carrying all 115 styles / 299 colourways on their launch dates.

Each deck is a single self-contained file — every photo is embedded, so it opens on
any laptop and keeps working from a download with no network.

- RTW: `S` for slide show, arrow keys to move, `F` full screen, `Esc` back to the dashboard
- ACC: `← →` / `Space` to move, `T` for the index of slide thumbnails, `F` full screen,
  `−` `+` to zoom and `0` back to fit; `#7` in the URL deep-links a slide, and the PDF
  button prints it one slide per page
- Both decks switch between English and 繁中 from the button in the control bar
  (文 / EN on RTW, 繁中 on ACC)

Rebuilt from source: RTW with `DECK_SLIDE_LIMIT=40` from
`src/service/fw26_product_training/render.py`, ACC from
`src/service/training_dashboard/build_dashboard.py`.
